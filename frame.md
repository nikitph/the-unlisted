# THE UNLISTED — approved visual direction
Photographic surveillance thriller in the visual language of Person of Interest, using the approved styleframe. Main character identity anchored to user portrait. Five fictional supporting subjects.
Palette: charcoal #080c0f, off-white #e9eeee, tracking yellow #e4cf38, alert red #ba3438. Generated photographs carry their own desaturated grading; do not add generic cinematic filters.
Display: Barlow Condensed SemiBold (SIL Open Font License). Telemetry: monospace, 18–24px (small machine annotations intentionally secondary). Titles 110px.
Full-bleed photographic plates, sharp rectangular crops, fine forensic marks. No rounded dashboard cards, blue/amber product UI, neon holograms, floating tiles or decorative glowing networks.
Motion: hard feed changes; calibrated camera zooms; scan-lock mechanism adapted from camera-scan-gate; discrete-text-sequence state changes; held final gaze. User approved imagery as the quality target. Generated still plates with designed camera and machine-vision animation; no claim of live-action character movement.
